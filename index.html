Sacred Places Nearby

Pandey's Homestay puts the holiest sites of Ayodhya right at your feet.

Saryu Ghat

~300 m · 4 min walk

Take the sacred Saryu snan at dawn. The river ghats are alive with morning aarti and the divine sounds of bells every day.

Get Directions

Nageshwar Nath Mandir

~200 m · 3 min walk

The ancient Shiva temple established by Kush, son of Lord Ram. One of the most revered temples in Ayodhya — walking distance from our stay.

Get Directions

Hanuman Gadhi

~1 km · 12 min walk

The famous hilltop temple of Lord Hanuman. Every devotee visiting Ayodhya must climb the 76 steps to seek Hanumanji's blessings first.

Get Directions

Ram Mandir

~2 km · 5 min by auto

The grand Ram Janmabhoomi Temple — the divine birthplace of Lord Ram. A short auto or e-rickshaw ride away from our homestay.

Get Directions

Kanak Bhawan

~2.5 km · 8 min by auto

The golden palace gifted by Kaikeyi to Sita Mata after marriage. A beautiful and spiritually significant temple adorned with gold ornaments.

Get Directions
